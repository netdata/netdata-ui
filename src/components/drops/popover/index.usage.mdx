# Popover

A floating panel that opens on hover or focus of its child and stays open while the pointer is over the target or the panel.

## When to use

- Show read-only detail that does not fit inline, e.g. the alerts raised on a node.
- Offer a few small controls the user can reach with the pointer.
- Don't use it for a short hint. Use `Tooltip`.
- Don't use it for choosing from a list. Use `Menu`.
- Don't use it for content that must be answered or dismissed. Use a modal.

## Placement

- `align` is the side of the target: `top` (default), `right`, `bottom` or `left`. The arrow points at the target.
- Pass `dropProps={{ align }}` for finer control, using `Drop` alignment.

## Content

- `content` is a node or a function that returns a node.
- By default content sits on the dropdown surface: bordered, 6px radius, padded, with an arrow.
- `plain` renders your content as is. Use it when the content draws its own surface.

<Canvas of={Stories.Custom} />

## States

- Opens on mouse over and focus of the target. Closes on mouse leave of both target and panel, blur, and Escape.
- `open` controls the state: `true` opens it, `false` closes it. Use it for always-open reviews and tests.
- `onHoverChange` reports when the pointer enters and leaves the target.
- The child gets an `aria-describedby` that points to the popover while it is open.
- `animation` fades and lifts it in; reduced motion removes the movement.

## Accessibility

- Give the child a focusable element, such as a button, so keyboard users can open it with focus.
- Content is described, not focus-trapped. Tab order continues from the target; keep interactive content minimal.

## Do and don't

- Do keep the panel narrow, around 200px wide, for readable lines.
- Do use theme colours in custom content.
- Don't depend on hover as the only way to reach important information.
- Don't open a second popover from inside the first.
