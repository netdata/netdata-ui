# Topbar

A header row with a title on the left and a group of actions on the right.

## When to use

- Use it at the top of a page, panel or room for a heading and its actions.
- Do not use it for tabbed navigation. Use Navigation instead.

## Props

- `mainChild`: content on the left, usually a heading.
- `children`: actions on the right, usually buttons.
- `extended`: widens the gap between actions.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## Spacing

- Default: actions are spaced by 5 units.
- Extended: actions are spaced by 8 units. Use it when the actions are icon buttons that need more air.

<Canvas of={Stories.Extended} />

## Accessibility

- Topbar adds no roles. Give icon-only buttons an accessible label.
- Put the heading in `mainChild` as a real heading component.

## Do and don't

- Do keep one primary action, placed last.
- Do use borderless neutral buttons for secondary icon actions.
- Don't put long content in `children`. The row does not wrap.
